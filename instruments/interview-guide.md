# Needfinding interview guide

Semi-structured, 15–25 min, remote voice call (WeChat), audio recorded.
Target: Chinese-speaking students or early-career researchers who are considering, or have gone through, moving abroad for study or work.
One or two interviews are enough: they triangulate the 431-comment corpus, they do not replace it. Depth beats count — follow up on anything concrete.

Before starting: send the consent form, get it signed, then repeat on tape: "本次通话会录音，用于我在特伦托大学的人机交互课程项目，引用会匿名，你随时可以退出。你同意吗？" Wait for an explicit yes.

Recording files: `P01.m4a`, `P02.m4a`, … matching the IDs in `screener-log.md`.

## A. Background (2 min)

1. What stage are you at, and what field?
   你现在处于什么阶段（本科/硕士/博士/工作），什么专业方向？
2. Are you planning to move abroad, or have you already? Which countries are on your list?
   你在考虑出国吗，还是已经出去了？目前在考虑哪些国家？

## B. Current decision process (8 min)

3. Walk me through the last time you seriously researched a destination. Where did you start?
   回想你上一次认真调研某个国家或学校，你是从哪里开始查的？
4. Which tools or sources did you actually use? (rankings, forums, xiaohongshu, seniors, agents)
   实际用了哪些工具或信息源？排名、论坛、小红书、学长学姐、中介？
5. What took the most time? What did you never manage to find out?
   最花时间的是什么？有什么信息是你一直没查到的？
6. Which sources do you distrust, and why?
   哪类信息你是不信的？为什么？

## C. Evidence needs (5 min)

7. Imagine two offers on the table. What data would actually change your choice?
   假设两个 offer 摆在面前，什么样的数据能真正影响你的选择？
8. How much does "probability of staying long-term" matter to you compared to school prestige?
   "长期留下来的概率"和"学校名气"相比，对你有多重要？
9. Would you trust statistics computed from scholars' career trajectories (where people actually moved and stayed)? What would make you trust or doubt them?
   如果有一个统计，基于学者真实履历（去了哪、留没留下），你会信吗？什么会让你信或者怀疑？

## D. Concept reaction (3 min)

Read this description, do not show the interface:
"有一个免费网站，你选定国籍后，它按国家、城市、机构三个层级告诉你：多少人到过那里、到了之后留在当地的比例、以及不再回母国的比例，数据来自公开的学者履历。"

10. What would you look up first?
    你会先查什么？
11. What would you worry about or double-check before trusting it?
    在相信它之前，你会担心或想核实什么？

## E. Wrap (2 min)

12. Anything about choosing where to go that we did not touch?
    关于"去哪"这个决定，还有什么我们没聊到的？

After the call: log the session in `screener-log.md`, save the recording, write 5–10 bullet notes per participant into `research/interviews/P0X.md` while it is fresh.
