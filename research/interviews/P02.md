## Decision process

- Starts with Google, then forums — Reddit study-abroad threads; sees agency posts on rednote/Douyin but would not hire an agent (01:08–01:59).
- Most time goes to verifying, not finding: "最花时间的其实不是查信息，是去证实信息……很多信息鱼龙混杂，有些是过时的" (02:12–02:28).
- The information he never found: outcomes. "很少能找到一个明确的统计数据，告诉我们读某个学校、某个专业，去向是怎么样的" (02:32–02:47). Distrusts schools' own salary dashboards: "学校官网发的那个 Power BI 报表，很大程度上都是灌水灌出来的" (02:54–03:04).
- Reflexively distrusts over-certain claims ("你读某个学校就一定会留下来") and prices that contradict common sense (03:15–03:46).

## Evidence needs

- Two-offer decision: budget and probability of staying come first; salary only as a tiebreaker, adjusted by local living costs (04:05–04:33).
- Stay probability vs prestige: retention matters more — "学校名气很难有一个相对客观的标准去评价" (04:36–04:49). Direct opposite of P01's prestige-first stance.
- Trusts trajectory-based statistics because they are data-grounded: "因为他是基于数据的，所以我倾向于相信他" (05:16–05:31).

## Concept reaction

- First lookup: stay rate, then arrival count — and he framed arrival as "对数据信度的检验，如果这个数据量很小，参考性不那么大" (05:55–06:20). Independently reinvented the sample-size requirement (FR8).
- Before trusting: would inspect which scholars are in the data; individual outcomes do not generalize (06:31–06:55).
- Unprompted addition: non-rational factors matter — political and cultural fit of the destination (Hong Kong vs US accounting PhD example, 07:03–07:54).
